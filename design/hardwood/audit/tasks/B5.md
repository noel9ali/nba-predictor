# B5 · Ticket notches: both notches centred on the perforation, at every width

## 1. Problem
The ticket's two half-circle notches should sit on the dashed perforation between stub and body. Today the top notch is
missing (covered), the bottom notch shows as a quarter circle offset right of the perforation, and on phones the
notches are covered too. Screenshots: `design/hardwood/audit/shots/B6-ticket-top-notch-missing.png`,
`design/hardwood/audit/shots/B6-ticket-bottom-notch-offset.png`.

## 2. Repro
`/?sample=1&scene=live` at 400, 768, 1440: scroll to `[data-testid=grid]`, wait 1.5 s (tickets slide in), zoom into the
top and bottom of the stub/body boundary of the first `.ticket`. Check a final ticket (`scene=final`) too.

## 3. Root cause
`public/static/css/components.css`:
- lines ~169–171: notches are radial-gradients in `.ticket`'s own background at `x = var(--stubw)` (96px),
  `y = 0 / 100%`. But `.stub` (line ~179, `background:var(--stub)`) paints over the left half of each notch, and the band
  (line ~186, `.band` with its own background and `margin:0 -22px`) paints over the top one. The perforation is the
  stub's `border-right:2px dashed` at x = 94–96px, so the gradient centre (96px) is also 1px right of the line's centre.
- Phone layout (`@media` at line ~224): the stub becomes a top strip (`border-bottom:2px dashed`) and the notch
  gradients move to `y = var(--stubh,58px)` on the left/right edges, but the stub (and its height when it wraps) again
  covers/misaligns them.
Designer note (`handoff/components/paper-ticket.md` ~line 100): `--stubh` hook exists; exact alignment is wanted.

## 4. Files owned
- `public/static/css/components.css` (only the `.ticket` / `.stub` notch rules)

## 5. Complete solution looks like
Pure CSS, drawn on the stub so they always follow the perforation, above the band:
- Remove the two notch radial-gradients from `.ticket`'s background (both desktop and the phone media query); keep
  `background: var(--stock)` and everything else.
- `.stub{position:relative; z-index:2}` (the ticket's click layer `.ticket__hit` is z-index 5, the stamp keeps its own
  z-index — check the stamp still renders above; if `.stamp` has no z-index, give the notches z-index 2 and confirm
  the stamp still overlaps the body correctly).
- Desktop (stub = left column): `.stub::before, .stub::after{content:"";position:absolute;width:18px;height:18px;
  border-radius:50%;background:var(--floor);right:-10px;pointer-events:none}` with `::before{top:-9px}`,
  `::after{bottom:-9px}`. The circle centre x = stub's right edge − 1px = centre of the 2px dashed border.
- Phone (same media query that turns the stub into a top strip): `right:auto; top:auto; bottom:-10px` and
  `::before{left:-9px}`, `::after{right:-9px; left:auto}` — centres on the ticket's left/right edges at the centre of the
  stub's 2px bottom border, whatever the stub's height (no `--stubh` needed).
- The half of each circle outside the ticket is floor-coloured on the floor, so it is invisible; it must not create
  horizontal page overflow at 320px (the page gutter is ≥ 16px > 9px — verify).
- `.ticket` has `box-shadow`; the notch circles sit above it, which is the intended "bite".
- No change to ticket size, stub content or animation (`.js .ticket.pre` translate). Reduced motion unchanged.

## 6. Out of scope
Stub height/dead space (D5), stub "PICK · NO BET" wrap (D6), team names (D7), "№" glyph (D11).

## 7. Acceptance tests (`tests/e2e/audit/B5.mjs`)
For w in 320, 400, 768, 1440, scenes live and final, first three tickets:
- Read `getComputedStyle(stub, '::before')` / `'::after'` (content not 'none') and compute the pseudo centres:
  desktop centre x = `stub.right + parseFloat(right offset)…` — simpler: compute from the rule:
  centre x of `::before` = stubRect.right − 1 (desktop) and assert the dashed border's centre is stubRect.right − 1
  (border-right-width 2px → centre = right − 1). Do this by reading computed `right`, `width` of the pseudo:
  `cx = stubRect.right - parseFloat(cs.right) - parseFloat(cs.width)/2` → must be within 1px of `stubRect.right − 1`.
  Top notch centre y = `stubRect.top + parseFloat(cs.top) + 9` within 1px of `ticketRect.top`; bottom one at `ticketRect.bottom`.
- Phone (≤ the media-query width): `::before` centre x within 1px of `ticketRect.left`, `::after` of `ticketRect.right`,
  both centre y within 1px of `stubRect.bottom − 1`.
- Visual: element screenshot of the first ticket's top and bottom edges; pixel at the notch centre (e.g. 2px inside the
  ticket) has the floor colour, not the stock colour (use `page.screenshot({clip})` + compare is optional; the geometry
  checks above are required).
- Audit "Accept when" — *Both notch centres within 1px of the perforation line at all widths.*
- `document.documentElement.scrollWidth <= innerWidth` at 320. `page.errors` empty.
