# B6 · Model step 7 mini ticket on phones: no broken tricode, no truncated stamp, logos like Tonight

## 1. Problem
On the Model page at 400px, the step-7 mini ticket's stub breaks "SAC" as "SA/C", the stamp text truncates
("$55.58 to win $52."), "−105 · FANDUEL" wraps, and the ticket has no team logos (Tonight tickets have them).
Screenshot: `design/hardwood/audit/shots/B7-step7-ticket-400.png`.

## 2. Repro
`/model?sample=1` at 400, 768, 1440; scroll to `#ch3 .step[data-step="7"]` (`scrollTo(p, '#ch3 .step[data-step="7"]', 2000)`).

## 3. Root cause
- `public/static/js/model/walkthrough.js` `gfxTicket(t)` lines ~376–397 builds `.mini-ticket` with `.stub`
  (`t.stubLabel` + `<b>t.stubTri</b>`, optional `t.stubMeta`), `.body` rows `.tm` (tricode + score), `.sp` spacer,
  `.mt-note`, and `.mini-stamp`. No logos. `t.stubMeta` is the "−105 · FANDUEL" line.
- `public/static/css/model.css` lines ~309–320: `.walk .mini-ticket{grid-template-columns:96px minmax(0,1fr)}`; the stub
  `b` is `font:400 28px/1 var(--jersey)` in a 96px column minus 24px padding → "SAC" doesn't fit at 400px and wraps;
  `.mini-stamp{position:absolute;right:22px;bottom:30px;font:30px}` overflows the narrow body and gets clipped.
- Tonight's tickets use `logo(tricode, 't-logo', 40)` from `public/static/js/logo.js` (returns an `<img>` or a chip).

## 4. Files owned
- `public/static/js/model/walkthrough.js` (only `gfxTicket` and the data it reads)
- `public/static/css/model.css` (only `.walk .mini-ticket*` and `.walk .mini-stamp*` rules)

## 5. Complete solution looks like
Match the Tonight paper ticket's look (prototype: `handoff/reference/prototypes/model.html`, step 7):
- Stub: tricode on one line (`white-space:nowrap`), font size `clamp(22px, 6vw, 28px)`; the meta line ("−105 ·
  FANDUEL") `white-space:nowrap`, and if it can't fit in the stub column, put the odds and the book on two lines
  (render them as two spans with a line break between, not a wrapped string).
- Body rows: each `.tm` gets the team logo before the tricode: `logo(tri, 'mt-logo', 28)` (import `logo` from
  `../logo.js`), 28px square, 10px gap, vertically centred. Logos for both rows.
- Stamp: all of its text visible at every width. Font `clamp(20px, 5.5vw, 30px)` for the word, `small` line below;
  allowed to wrap to two lines inside the stamp (`white-space:normal; max-width: calc(100% - 24px)`), never clipped by
  the ticket. Keep its rotation (−9deg), double border and colours. At 1440 it looks as before.
- At 400 the ticket fits the column (max-width 520px unchanged), no element overflows `.mini-ticket` horizontally.
- Reduced motion: no animation involved (stamp is static on the Model page).

## 6. Out of scope
Tonight tickets, other walkthrough steps, notches on the mini ticket.

## 7. Acceptance tests (`tests/e2e/audit/B6.mjs`)
For w in 320, 400, 768, 1440:
- Stub tricode element (`.mini-ticket .stub b`): `getClientRects().length === 1` (one line) and text is 'SAC'.
- Every element inside `.mini-stamp`: `scrollWidth <= clientWidth + 1`; the stamp's rect is inside `.mini-ticket`'s
  rect (±1px, after rotation use getBoundingClientRect); the stamp's text includes the full `small` text from the data
  (e.g. contains '$52.93' if the sample's stamp sub is '+$52.93' — read it from the DOM text and check it is not cut:
  `small.scrollWidth <= small.clientWidth + 1`).
- `.mini-ticket .tm img, .mini-ticket .tm .mt-logo` count === 2.
- The meta line element(s): each `getClientRects().length === 1`.
- No horizontal overflow; `page.errors` empty.
- Audit "Accept when" — *No truncated stamp text; "SAC" on one line.*
