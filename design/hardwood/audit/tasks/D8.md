# D8 · "Last 33 bets" card: no stretch, even rows (11 × 3)

## 1. Problem
At 1440 the "Last 33 bets" card is stretched to the calibration card's height (645px) and mostly empty, and its
squares wrap unevenly (25 + 8). Screenshot: `design/hardwood/audit/shots/D8-last33-empty-card.png`.

## 2. Repro
`/?sample=1&scene=live` and `scene=early` (fewer bets) at 400, 768, 1440: `scrollTo(p, '.mgrid', 1500)`.

## 3. Root cause
`public/static/css/tonight.css`:
- ~103: `.mgrid{display:grid;grid-template-columns:minmax(0,1.6fr) minmax(0,1fr);…}` — default `align-items:stretch`.
- ~140–145: `.wl{display:flex;flex-wrap:wrap;gap:4px}` with `22×26` squares → as many per row as fit.

## 4. Files owned
- `public/static/css/tonight.css` (only `.mgrid` and `.wl …` rules)

## 5. Complete solution looks like
- `.mgrid{align-items:start}` — each card is as tall as its content (the calibration card stays as is).
- `.wl{display:grid; grid-template-columns:repeat(11, 22px); gap:4px; justify-content:start}` — 33 bets = 3 rows of 11,
  fewer bets fill rows of 11 left to right. Square size, colours, W/L styles and the pop-in animation (`--k` stagger)
  unchanged. At 320px the 11 columns (11×22 + 10×4 = 282px) must fit the card's content width — verify; if not, use
  `repeat(11, minmax(0, 22px))` so squares shrink slightly instead of overflowing.
- ≤ 900px the cards stack (existing rule) — unchanged.

## 6. Out of scope
season.js (`renderWL`), the calibration card (C1).

## 7. Acceptance tests (`tests/e2e/audit/D8.mjs`)
For w in 320, 400, 768, 1440, scene live:
- `[data-wl-card]` height ≤ its content height + padding (`card.scrollHeight === card.clientHeight` and
  card height < 260px at 1440).
- `.wl i` count = 33; their tops form exactly 3 distinct rows with 11 each.
- Every square is inside the card; no horizontal overflow.
- scene early: rows of 11 then the remainder.
- `page.errors` empty.
