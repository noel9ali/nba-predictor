# D10 · Glance rows ≤ 900px: teams above the court, court full width

## 1. Problem
Between 761px and 900px the glance court rows keep the 3-column layout (200px label | court | 170px status), so the
court is narrow and the edge tags crowd the team boxes. Screenshot: `design/hardwood/audit/shots/C6-glance-768.png`.

## 2. Repro
`/?sample=1&scene=live`, `scene=final`, `scene=edges` at 768, 820, 900, 901, 1440 → `[data-testid=chart] .gl`.

## 3. Root cause
`public/static/css/tonight.css` ~17: `.gl{grid-template-columns:200px minmax(0,1fr) 170px}`; the stacked layout
(`@media (max-width:760px){ .gl{grid-template-columns:minmax(0,1fr) auto} .gl .court-strip{grid-column:1/-1;grid-row:2} .gl__lab{flex-direction:row;…} }`)
starts only at 760px.

## 4. Files owned
- `public/static/css/tonight.css` (only the `.gl …` media query at ~30–35)

## 5. Complete solution looks like
- Change that media query from `max-width:760px` to `max-width:900px` (same rules inside): label and status on row 1,
  the court spanning the full row on row 2.
- Check the label row at 768–900: the label row (`.gl__lab` as a row: who / pick+edge / sub) and the status stay on one
  line or wrap cleanly (no overlap with the status column).
- > 900px: unchanged. ≤ 760px: unchanged (same rules).
- Populate animation (T1–T4) and reduced motion unchanged.

## 6. Out of scope
Animation timings (B7), glance.js.

## 7. Acceptance tests (`tests/e2e/audit/D10.mjs`)
For w in 768, 820, 900: every `.gl .court-strip` width ≥ 90% of its `.gl` row width, and its top ≥ the bottom of
`.gl__lab` and `.gl__st` (row 2). `.gl__lab` and `.gl__st` rects don't intersect. Court `.tag` rects stay inside the
court-strip rect. At 901 and 1440: 3-column layout (court left > label right). `page.errors` empty.

Diff, test output, element screenshots of `[data-testid=chart]`: `D10-before-<w>` / `D10-<w>` at 400/768/1440.
