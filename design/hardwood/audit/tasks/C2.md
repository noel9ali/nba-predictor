## 5. Complete solution looks like
- Chapter variant measures its host: `W = min(560, max(300, host.clientWidth))`, `H = round(W × 380/560)` (min 240).
  Give the SVG `width`/`height` attributes = W/H so it renders 1:1 (text at its CSS size, 11px). `initCalibration`
  re-renders when the plot host's width changes by > 8px (ResizeObserver, debounced 100ms, same pattern as Tonight's
  `mountModelCard`).
- Padding scales but keeps room for labels: `pad = { l: 58, r: 16, t: 16, b: 54 }`; y tick labels right-anchored at
  `pad.l − 8`; x tick labels at `H − pad.b + 18`; caption at `H − 6` (≥ 12px below the tick labels). Add the y title
  `Actually won` rotated −90°, as in the card (C1), left of the y ticks.
- On narrow widths (W < 420) the caption may be shortened to `Model said · dot size and label = picks` so it fits
  within W − 8px; it must never be clipped or overflow.
- "perfect": along the dashed diagonal near its top-right end, rotated to the diagonal's on-screen angle, 4–8px above
  the line, anchor `end`. If its box intersects any dot or `.lb` label box (measure with `getBBox()` after inserting
  the SVG), slide it towards the origin along the diagonal in 10px steps until clear.
- Count labels `.lb`: keep beside their dots; if a label would leave the plot on the right, flip it to the left (exists);
  no label may intersect another text or leave the SVG.
- At 1440 the chart looks like today apart from the label fixes (W = 560 when the column allows).
- No animation on this chart (M9); reduced motion unchanged.
