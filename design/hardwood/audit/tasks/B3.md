# B3 · One consistent aria-label for every ticket button

## 1. Problem
Ticket buttons (`.ticket__hit`) have inconsistent labels: final tickets get "BOS at NYK, final 104–110, Cashed +$38.18,
open details"; others just "BOS at NYK, open details" (no time, pick, stake, odds, model vs market).

## 2. Repro
`/?sample=1&scene=live`, `scene=nextup`, `scene=final`, `scene=nobets`, `scene=before`, past `/?sample=1&date=2026-11-16`
at 1440 (labels are width-independent). Read `[...document.querySelectorAll('.ticket__hit')].map(b => b.getAttribute('aria-label'))`.
Replay: labels must update after `__sampleFeed.step()`.

## 3. Root cause
`public/static/js/tonight/tickets.js` `ariaLabel(g, info)` lines ~63–68 builds only "<away> at <home>" plus a
result when stamped. Called from `createTicket()` and `updateTicket()`.

## 4. Files owned
- `public/static/js/tonight/tickets.js` (only `ariaLabel` and new pure helpers it needs)

## 5. Complete solution looks like
One builder. Parts joined by '. ', ending with 'Open details.' Use the page's own formatters (`fmt.time`,
`fmt.money`, `fmt.odds`, `fmt.pct`) so the numbers match the ticket. Plain text (it's an attribute; no esc needed).
1. Matchup + time/status: `BOS at NYK, 7:30 PM ET` (scheduled; `Time TBD` if no tip time), `BOS at NYK, live, Q3 4:12`
   (live; clock if known, else just `live`), `BOS at NYK, final 104–110` (final with scores; away–home with an en dash),
   `BOS at NYK, postponed`, `BOS at NYK, void`.
2. Pick/bet: with a bet `Bet on NYK, $42.10 at −120`; pick without bet `Pick NYK, no bet`; pending `Pick pending`.
3. Model vs market when both known: `Model 61.0% vs market 55.0%` (fmt.pct, one decimal, as the ticket's "Model ·
   market" line shows).
4. Status/result when known: live with a pick → the margin label (`NYK up 6`); stamped → `info.label`
   (`Cashed +$38.18`, `Lost −$42.10`, `Pick right, no bet`, `Void`), plus `, unofficial` when the stamp shows it.
5. `Open details.`
Example: `BOS at NYK, 7:30 PM ET. Bet on NYK, $42.10 at −120. Model 61.0% vs market 55.0%. Open details.`
- The visible ticket is unchanged. The glance rows' labels (glance.js) are out of scope.

## 6. Out of scope
glance.js row labels, drawer, ticket markup.

## 7. Acceptance tests (`tests/e2e/audit/B3.mjs`)
For each scene above at 1440, every `.ticket__hit` label:
- matches `/^[A-Z]{2,4} at [A-Z]{2,4}, (\d{1,2}:\d{2} [AP]M ET|Time TBD|live(, .+)?|final \d+–\d+|final|postponed|void)\. /`
- ends with `Open details.`
- contains `Bet on <TRI>, $` when the ticket shows a "Bet on" stub, `Pick <TRI>, no bet` for "Pick · no bet" stubs.
- contains `Model \d+\.\d% vs market \d+\.\d%` whenever the ticket's lines show a model/market pair.
- final scene: every label contains one of `Cashed`, `Lost`, `Pick right`, `Pick wrong`, `Void`.
- no label contains a hyphen between digits (/\d-\d/).
- replay at 1440: after 6 steps, labels reflect the new states (at least one label changed to 'final').
- Audit "Accept when" — *All ticket buttons match the pattern.*
- `page.errors` empty.
