# D5 · No dead space at the bottom of tickets

## 1. Problem
At ≥ 561px, tickets are ~354px tall but the body's content ends ~94px above the bottom ("MIA AT ORL" foot, then empty
paper). Screenshot: `design/hardwood/audit/shots/D5-ticket-dead-space.png`.

## 2. Repro
`/?sample=1&scene=live` and `scene=final` at 768 and 1440 (and 400, where the stub is a top strip — must stay unchanged).
Scroll to `[data-testid=grid]`, wait 1.6s.

## 3. Root cause (measured at 1440)
The stub column sets the ticket height: stub fields end at 209px, then `gap:14px`, then the vertical
"Ticket № 0190" (`.stub .no`, `writing-mode:vertical-rl`, `margin-top:auto`, 113px tall), plus 18px padding = 354px.
The body's content needs only 260px + 18px padding = 278px.
`public/static/css/components.css` ~179–183 (`.stub`, `.stub .no`), ~185 (`.ticket__body`), ~239 (`.stamp{right:26px;bottom:66px}`).
B5 (done earlier) gave `.stub` `position:relative; z-index:2` and the notch pseudo-elements — keep them.

## 4. Files owned
- `public/static/css/components.css` (only `.stub .no`, `.ticket__body`, `.stamp` position rules)

## 5. Complete solution looks like
- Take the vertical ticket number out of the stub's flow so it no longer sets the height:
  `.stub .no{position:absolute; right:10px; bottom:18px; margin:0}` — vertical as now (same writing mode, rotation,
  font, letter-spacing), running up the stub's right side next to the perforation, where the fields (left-aligned,
  ≤ ~60px wide) never reach. The stub's flow height is then the fields only.
- The ticket height becomes `max(body content, stub fields + number room)`: also give the stub
  `min-height: calc(<number length> + 36px)` is NOT needed if the number fits beside the fields; verify no overlap:
  the `.no` rect must not intersect any other stub child's rect, at 768 and 1440, for bet / pass / pending stubs.
- Dead space: the distance from the last body child's bottom to the ticket's bottom ≤ 20px (the body's 18px padding)
  for every ticket at 768/1440, in live and final scenes.
- Stamp (final tickets): with the shorter ticket, re-check `.stamp{bottom:66px}`: it must not overlap `.match` (the team
  rows and scores) or `.foot`. If it does, set `bottom` so the stamp sits in the band between `.match` and `.foot`
  (where the hidden `.lines` are). Keep its right offset and rotation.
- ≤ 560px (stub as a top strip, `.stub .no{display:none}`): unchanged.
- The slide-in (`.pre`) and stamp-land (`thunk`) animations unchanged; reduced motion unchanged.

## 6. Out of scope
Notches (B5), "PICK · NO BET" wrap (D6), team names (D7), "№" text (D11).
